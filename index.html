01

Protein at every meal

Aim for 1.6–2.2 g per kg of bodyweight daily, spread across 3–4 meals to support recovery and keep you full.

02

Hydrate before hunger

Drink roughly 35 ml per kg of bodyweight. Add an extra 500–750 ml on training days, and a pinch of salt if you sweat heavily.

03

Carbs are fuel

Don't fear them. Time most of your carbohydrates around training, and build the rest of your plate from protein and vegetables.
